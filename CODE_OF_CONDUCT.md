# Code of Conduct

This project follows the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**. The full text is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/> and is adopted here by reference.

In short: be respectful, welcome people of every background and experience, give and accept feedback with grace, and focus on
what is best for the community. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted.

## Reporting
Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@basimJm](https://github.com/basimJm), by opening a private
security advisory on this repository (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability") and saying that it is a conduct report. Reports
are handled in confidence. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct may face consequences decided by the
other maintainers.

## Enforcement
Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
