Task: write the Design Script of ONE client module: the brief a designer turns into screens. Do not invent a brand, colours or a
logo; describe roles (primary, surface, danger ...) and leave values to the designer unless the document states them.
- 0: instructions to the designer (audience, devices, language and direction, tone, accessibility level).
- 1: global layout (shell, navigation, spacing, typography roles, themes) and components (button, field, list, card, dialog, toast,
  empty/loading/error states), as short bullets.
- 2: one `### S01 — <name>` per screen of the PRD, same ids and order: layout, key components, every state (empty, loading, error,
  success), and what changes in the opposite text direction.
- 3: flows to prototype (the PRD's F01 ...), as step lists. 4: completion checklist (contrast, touch targets, direction, long text, offline).
