SCHOOL FEE PORTAL: NOTES FROM THE BOARD MEETING (Hillview Academy, Nairobi)

Problem: parents pay fees in cash at the bursar's office or by bank deposit slip and then argue about balances. The bursar
spends two weeks every term reconciling. We want a website where parents see what they owe and pay with mobile money.
It must be a website only. No mobile app: parents already use their phones' browsers and the school will not publish
apps. It must work well on a cheap phone and a slow connection.

People
* Parent or guardian: logs in, sees each child (up to 5), the fee statement per term (tuition, transport, lunch, activities),
  pays all or part of it, downloads a receipt as PDF, and can see payment history.
* Bursar: the accountant of the school. Sets the fee structure per class and term, applies discounts and bursaries, records
  cash or bank payments that happen outside the website, reconciles, and exports to the accounting software as CSV.
* Head teacher: read only dashboard of collection rates per class, and a list of the biggest arrears.
* Class teacher: can see only which children in their class have cleared fees, to give out exam cards. No amounts.
* Our IT contractor: manages user accounts.

Money
Currency Kenyan shillings (KES). No decimals needed in practice, but the accountant wants cents recorded. Payment through mobile
money (STK push to the parent's phone). Each payment gets a receipt number. Part payments allowed; the minimum is KES 500.
Fees are due 14 days before the term opens. A late fee of 2% is added after 30 days overdue, only if the board has approved
it for that term. Overpayments carry forward to the next term. Siblings get a 10% discount on tuition for the second child onward.

States
Invoice: draft, issued, partly paid, paid, overdue, cancelled. Payment: initiated, confirmed, failed, reversed.

Reminders
SMS to parents 7 days before due date, on the due date, and 7 days after. Receipt by SMS and e-mail. Weekly e-mail to the head
teacher with the collection summary.

Languages and place
English and Kiswahili. Left to right. Kenya only. Times in East Africa Time.

What exists
A student list and class lists in a spreadsheet maintained by the secretary. The bursar uses a desktop accounting package that we
keep. No existing website except a brochure page on a free hosting service.

Open points
* Which mobile money provider and which bank holds the school account (finance committee decides next month).
* Do parents from the boarding section pay a different structure?
* Who reverses a payment made by mistake: the bursar alone, or the head teacher as well?
* How long we keep records (the accountant says seven years).
