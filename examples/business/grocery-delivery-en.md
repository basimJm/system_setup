Subject: Green Basket Market, what we want built

Hi,

Here is the long version of what I told you on the phone. We run one grocery shop in Dublin and want to start delivering
to the neighbourhood. Later maybe a second shop.

What customers do
People open an app on their phone (Android and iPhone), browse about 1,500 products by aisle, add to a basket, choose a delivery
slot (two-hour windows, 8:00 to 20:00, same day if they order before 15:00), pay by card, and track the order. They can repeat
a previous order with one tap. Some products are sold by weight (fruit, deli): the customer orders 1 kg and we pack 960 g to
1.1 kg, so the final price is set at the scale and the card is charged the real amount, never more than 10% above what they saw.

What we do in the shop
Staff use a tablet at the packing bench: they see new orders in the order they have to be picked, tick items off, weigh the
loose ones, mark substitutions (the customer chose in advance "replace with similar" or "do not replace"), and mark the order
ready. A shop manager sets prices, offers and stock, and can switch products off when they run out. This must be a website,
not an app, because the tablets are shared and I do not want anybody installing things.

Drivers
Two part-time drivers with their own cars. They need an app to see their route, open the address in a maps application, and
mark delivered with a photo of where the bags were left. We do not need live tracking of the driver on a map in the first version.

Orders
Placed, being packed, ready, out for delivery, delivered, or cancelled. A customer can cancel until packing starts. If we cannot
deliver the customer gets a full refund. Delivery fee is 3.50 euro, free above 60 euro. Minimum basket 20 euro.
Delivery area is a list of postcodes (about 12), nothing outside.

Money
Euro, cents. Card only, charged when the order is packed (we authorise at checkout). VAT is included in prices; some food
is zero-rated and some is 13.5%, the accountant will tell us the rules per product category. I want a daily sales
sheet by e-mail to me and the accountant.

Messages
Text message with a code to log in. Push notification when the order is packed and when the driver is on the way. E-mail receipt.
If the customer is not at home the driver calls them.

Other
Everything in English for now, maybe Polish later because many of our customers are Polish. We already have a product list in
a spreadsheet with barcodes and photos in a shared folder. No website yet. Our supplier prices change weekly.

Not sure yet
Whether to charge for substitutions when the replacement is more expensive. How to handle alcohol (ID check at the door?).
Whether we need loyalty points.
